It's been `214 days` since my last post.

**I'm back.**

---

After I finished school, I took a step back from just about everything I'd been doing in my spare time — including this website. It wasn't a conscious decision, and it wasn't a grand plan. Life after graduation simply pulled me in a million different directions, and the blog, quietly, was the first thing to fall by the wayside.

I kept reading, I kept tinkering, and I kept thinking about posts I wanted to write. But sitting down and actually writing them? That never quite happened. Weeks turned into months, and suddenly the gap between posts was longer than I'd like to admit.

So what's bringing me back? The same thing that got me writing again last year: [**Blaugust**](https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/15/blaugust-2026-is-coming/).

<Callout2 title="What is Blaugust?" variant="note" defaultOpen={true}>
  **Blaugust** is a month-long event where bloggers challenge themselves to post more often (31 posts for the 31 days in August) and engage with the Blaugust community.

  Check out the [Blaugust 2026 announcement](https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/15/blaugust-2026-is-coming/) and the [calendar with weekly prompts & group projects](https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/07/23/blaugust-2026-calendar-weekly-prompts-group-projects/) for more info.
</Callout2>

## In memory of Belghast

Blaugust is the brain child of Mark Temple, known across the blogging community as [Belghast](https://aggronaut.com/) of Tales of the Aggronaut. Sad as it is to write this, we lost him on July 2, 2026 — and the Blaugust Discord mentors decided to carry the event forward this year in his honour.

I only got to know Blaugust last year, through a community I was brand new to, but even in that short time it was obvious how much of the event's spirit came from Belghast. I never interacted with him directly, yet the warmth of the community he built spoke for itself. I'm glad the mentors kept the tradition alive this year — it means a lot to writers like me who owe their blogging revival to this event.

## Why this time will stick

Last year I joined Blaugust with only half the month left and told myself I'd write at least once a week. I ended up publishing a handful of posts and a retrospective, and honestly? It was enough to remind me how much I enjoy this — enough to carry the habit into September and beyond, until life caught up with me.

This year I'm starting on day two instead of day fifteen. No two-posts-a-day heroics planned — I'd rather publish words I'm proud of than pad the calendar with filler. But I do want to:

- Write about the things I've been mulling over during the break, including some longer posts that have been sitting in my drafts for months
- Respond to posts from other Blaugust participants, which is how I discovered some of my favourite blogs last year
- Keep the momentum going past August this time — a lesson I clearly needed to learn twice

## Closing thoughts

I don't know how many posts I'll get out this month, and I'm trying to be okay with that. Showing up again — after a gap I let stretch far too long — is its own kind of win. The [Blaugust 2026 participant list](https://nerdgirlthoughts.game.blog/2026/08/01/blaugust-2026-meet-the-mentors-participant-list/) is already full of familiar names from last year, and I'm looking forward to reading what everyone writes this month.

**Here's to another month of words. Welcome back, me.**
