## My favourite TV shows of 2025

<TraktGrid

- **Andor** season 2 was hands-down one of the most incredible TV experiences I've had. Truly a masterpiece of storytelling. 
- **The Studio** was really fun, went in with no expectations and was pleasantly surprised. 
- **Parks and Recreation** and **Brooklyn Nine-Nine** are comfort shows that I'm still going through (slowly, because I don't want to see them ending anytime soon). 
- I found myself enjoying season 2 of **Peacemaker** way more than I thought I would (it was nice to see the focus on the characters, and I'm always up for something with good banter). 
- **Person of Interest** was something I put on the background a lot while doing something else, but I grew to enjoy the story; it does a good job of integrating a larger story into it's procedural format. Also rather timely with it's themes around surveillance and AI. 
- I didn't realize I watched **Mr Bates vs The Post Office** this year, but it deserves to be on the list because it's such a wild true story.

## My favourite movies of 2025

Unfortunately, I found 2025 a bit of a so-so year for movies. There were some good ones (I thoroughly enjoyed **Superman**, **Sinners**, and **A Minecraft Movie**), but nothing that really blew me away. 

And yes, **A Minecraft Movie**, **Thunderbolts**, **Fight or Flight**, **The Bad Guys 2** are indeed among my favourite movies of the year. The only thing I care about when picking my favourites is how much I enjoyed watching them, and all of these movies delivered on that front.
