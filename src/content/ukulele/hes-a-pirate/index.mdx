The iconic "He's a Pirate" theme from *Pirates of the Caribbean: The
Curse of the Black Pearl* — a great one for practicing moving between
the C and E strings with a steady, driving strum.

Play it with a consistent quarter-note pulse: down, down, down, down.
The melody stays right in first position, so there's nothing to reach
for — focus on keeping the rhythm punchy like the film score. The
chromatic bits (cis', gis') are the seasick turns in the melody, so
don't let them trip you up.
